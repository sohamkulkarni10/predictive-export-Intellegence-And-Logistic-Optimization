# Destination Market Notes for Indian Agri Exports

## Bangladesh
Strong recurring demand for wheat and some staples. Short sea transit from east-coast India ports (Kolkata/Haldia/Paradip) usually wins on cost and time. Watch local tender and import-growth news.

## Saudi Arabia / UAE / Gulf
Import-dependent food markets. West-coast Indian ports are typically efficient. Packaging, Halal-related process requirements, and cold-chain (for some produce) can matter. UAE CEPA may improve preferential access for eligible goods.

## Germany / Netherlands / EU
Higher SPS and traceability expectations. Longer transit, often via transshipment. Freight and compliance cost more; prioritize only when demand score and margin are strong.

## Vietnam / Indonesia / Malaysia / Singapore
SE Asia lanes favor east/south Indian ports depending on origin. Coffee, spices, cotton, and feed grains appear in regional trade flows. Check ASEAN-related origin rules where relevant.

## Sri Lanka / Nepal
Shorter regional routes. Nepal often multimodal inland. Policy and payment terms can be as important as freight.

## Japan / China
Quality specs and documentation discipline are critical. Transit longer; container priority should require solid demand evidence.
